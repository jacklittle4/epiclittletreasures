Privacy

Simple privacy policy.

Epic Little Treasures uses contact forms so customers can ask questions, send shipping details, and request custom work.

Information collected

Forms may collect your name, email, phone number, item interest, shipping details, and message.

How it is used

Information is used to reply to questions, fill and ship orders (including your bundle preferences), and discuss custom orders.

Payments

Card, Klarna, and Affirm payments are processed by Stripe; this site never sees or stores your card number. Cash App and Venmo payments go through those apps. Each has its own privacy policy.

Analytics

The site uses no tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Your cart is saved only in your own browser until you check out.
