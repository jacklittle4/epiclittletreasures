Helpful answers

A few simple answers before you buy or send a message.

Handmade work is personal, and ordering should feel easy. These answers keep the basics clear.

Do you teach classes or workshops?

Yes! Stephanie hosts relaxed paper-craft and junk journal teaching sessions. Check the Workshops page for the next class, or send a message to ask what is coming up.

How do the junk journal bundles work?

Bundles come in 10, 15, 20, and 25 pulls, plus the EPIC EPIC bundle of 20 envelopes. Current prices are on the Shop page, and every bundle includes free shipping.

How do I buy something?

Tap Add to cart on any bundle (add as many as you like), open your cart, answer 4 quick questions so Stephanie can hand-pick your pulls, and check out. Custom work starts with a quick message through the contact form.

Are the prices final?

Fixed-price catalog items can be paid for as listed. Custom work can change with size, materials, detail, quantity, and timing.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes. Custom journals, paper sets, and gift bundles can start with a simple idea, color palette, occasion, budget, and deadline.

Are pieces one of a kind?

Yes. The shop describes everything as hand stitched, hand dyed, handmade, and one of a kind, so materials and finishing details vary.

Where do I see the newest work?

TikTok is the best place for live sales and current work. Facebook, email, and the contact form are good for follow-up questions.

What should I include in my order message?

Include item name, quantity, color preferences, occasion, budget comfort, deadline, pickup or shipping needs, and whether it is a gift.

Does the site take payments?

Yes. Checkout is handled securely by Stripe — pay by card, or split it into payments with Klarna or Affirm. Cash App and Venmo are welcome too.

Can I return a handmade item?

One-of-a-kind handmade pieces are generally final sale. If something is wrong with the order, send a message so the shop can follow up.

How long does shipping take?

Ready-made items can usually be prepared faster than custom work. Send shipping details after payment so timing can be confirmed.

Still have a question?

Send a quick note and Stephanie will get back to you.
